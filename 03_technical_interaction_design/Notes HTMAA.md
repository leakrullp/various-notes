# Basic Electronics 
Some basics terms when dealing with electricity are:
- **Ampére**: how much electrical charge is passing a certain point per second
- **Volt**: how strongly electricity is pushed through the circuit
- **Watt**: is a unit of power and it is the product of Ampere multiplied by Volt
$$
P_{(Watts)} = I_{(Amps)} \cdot V_{(Volts)}
$$

### Ohm's Law
Ohm's law is $V=I \cdot R$ and the units mean the following:
$$
V_{(Potential/volts)} = I_{(Current/amps)} \cdot R_{(Resistance)}
$$
